Numbers become a strip, not a sentence Views, likes, downloads and placements sit in one bordered strip with the count above the label — scannable at a glance, and the likes cell is the like button itself (it fills and counts up on tap). Everything today's card shows is still here; nothing is an emoji run.
One primary action Add to my room is the only dark button. Like and Download sit beside it as outlines; on mobile they compress to 56px icon squares so the primary keeps its full width.
Report stays quiet A plain underlined link until you need it, then an inline panel with the four reasons — inappropriate, spam, stolen, other — Send, and a line saying it goes to a human. Once sent it becomes a settled “Reported” pill; it can't be sent twice.
Owner mode Switch the role above to Owner: public/private is a visible switch with its consequence spelled out, name and description are editable with counters (80 / 500), categories show as locked chips, and delete asks once before it means it.
Toova models Built-ins drop like, report and download — there's nothing to like or flag — leaving the model, its measurements and one button. The card shape doesn't change, so nothing jumps around between models.
