One rail Add, Room look, Light, Pieces — four verbs, always in the same place. The topbar keeps only file-level actions; everything else moved out of it.
One context bar Advanced / Bedding / Customize collapse into one “Edit details” inspector with plain tabs — Size & fit, Bedding, Finish — that adapt to the piece.
Camera made visible A permanent view puck with named presets and a spelled-out drag / scroll / pan legend, plus Present mode for showing the room off.
Leaves, strings, free lights Not a separate tool rail — a “Draw it in the room” block at the top of the Add sheet, next to the library you're already browsing. Picking one drops you straight into anchor-clicking (Draw lights); the sag, spacing and palette live in the same Edit-details inspector as everything else (Strand).
Ceiling lights Moved out of Room look → Shell and into Light under “Fixtures in this room”, above the strands and free lights you've made. Everything that emits light is in one place; Room look stays about surfaces.
Raising a piece Three ways, no panel: a ▲▼ handle beside the selection with a live inch readout and a dotted drop line to the floor, a Height stepper in the context bar, and ⌥↑/↓ (⇧drag in the room). See Lift.
Bringing a piece in One page, three routes — upload a GLB, generate from a photo, or make a poster — with the requirements standing beside the form instead of appearing as errors after you submit (Import).
