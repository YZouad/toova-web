The designer, made legible

A redesign of the room designer and its 3D view — fewer entry points, one place for every decision.

August 2026 Desktop · key panel states
Where it broke down

Three sources of confusion

01

Four competing surfaces

Topbar, right rail, floating quick-bar and slide-in panels all offered a way in.

02

Opaque panel names

Advanced, Bedding, Customize and Room look gave no clue what each one governed.

03

Hidden camera controls

Orbit, zoom and pan were never stated, so a lost view stayed lost.

Four rules for the rebuild

First run and the walkthrough

Screen 02 · selection

Selection reads in the scene

The piece labels itself with its real dimensions. Snap guides say what it caught, and paired rotate handles sit next to it.

Screen 03 · piece details

Three panels became one

Advanced, Bedding and Customize collapse into a single inspector with tabs named for what they change.

Size & fit — scale, rotation, wall mount Bedding — beds only Finish — colors and materials
Screen 04 · add a piece

The model library

Screen 05 · room look

Room-wide, and it says so

Paint, flooring and trim carry a one-line scope note: changing the look never moves your furniture.

Light and mood lives beside it as its own entry, since nothing it changes is ever saved to the list.

Screen 06 · search

One search over everything

Pieces and actions in the same list, with a visible topbar entry so it is discoverable without the shortcut.

Screen 07 · camera

Camera stated, then presented

Room, Desk and Top presets with a reset, over a legend that spells out drag, scroll and pan. Present mode clears every editing control.

Summary

What changed

Before After
Topbar, right rail, quick-bar, panels One left rail of four verbs
Advanced · Bedding · Customize One inspector, tabs per piece
Thumbnail gallery drawer Library sheet: source, filter, sort
Undocumented camera Presets, reset and a legend
No first-run guidance Four-step walkthrough, dismissible
Open questions

What we need next

Mobile — the rail and the library sheet both need a small-screen answer. Real catalog counts and category coverage per source. Whether Present mode should produce a shareable link.
