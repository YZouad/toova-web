2a Designer on a phone — dock, sheets, thumb-sized handles
Same four verbs as 1a, re-seated for one hand: the rail becomes a bottom dock, panels become snap-height sheets, and every control that used to be a 28px target is now 44px or larger. Each screen below is live — tap around inside them.
Roomnothing selected
Room fills the screen. Dock sits in the thumb arc; camera chip and the $412 checklist pill ride the top corners; the gesture legend is spelled out, once.
Piece selectedtap a piece
The dock swaps for an action sheet: size, height, rotate, copy, remove, and one dark button into details. Rotate and lift also sit in-scene as 46px pucks beside the piece.
Raising a piece42″ up
▲▼ puck, a stepper in the sheet, or long-press and drag up. The dotted line to the floor and the inch chip stay visible while it floats, so nothing looks broken.
Addtall sheet
Search, three sources, categories as a scrolling chip row instead of a sidebar, and one-column rows big enough to hit. Draw-your-own and “bring in your own piece” pin to the footer.
Checklist$412 pill
The pill opens a full sheet, not a dropdown: progress ring, To place / Placed / All, and rows grouped by zone with sticky headers — it scrolls to any length. Each row shows how many options exist and the cheapest one, with a Shop pill as the tap target.
Shopping one item5 options
Tapping a row replaces the sheet with that item's options — price, retailer and ship time up front. Shop is the 54px accent button; Place in room, Add to list and GLB sit under it. Sort by popular or price; back returns to the checklist.
Drawing lightstap to place
Instructions at the top where a thumb never covers them, Cancel / Undo / Finish across the bottom at 52px. Tap the room to drop anchors; the swag previews as you go.
Lightfixtures live here
Time of day, brightness, then Fixtures: the ceiling-lights switch, the strands you've drawn, and the two draw buttons. Room look stays about surfaces.
Edit detailsthree tabs
One sheet, the same adaptive tabs as desktop. Swatches are 46px, steppers 40px, sliders have a 30px hit band — no pinching to hit a duvet color.
Bring a piece inroute first
Phone leads with the camera, because that's the thing a phone has and a laptop doesn't. Three routes, then one form — never three tabs of fields on a 390px screen.
From a photocamera route
Framing guide, capture, then a job card you can walk away from. Name, categories and the measured inches follow underneath in one scroll.
Postersize, then crop
Print size comes first because it locks the crop shape; the frame then fills the sheet, panned by drag and zoomed by pinch — the one place a phone beats the desktop.
Presentshare the room
All chrome gone but one bar: previous, current view, next, and Edit. Tapping the room hides even that — this is the screen you hand to a roommate.
Rail → dock Add, Room look, Light, Pieces keep their order and labels, so muscle memory carries between devices. The dock hides whenever a sheet or a selection owns the bottom of the screen.
Panels → sheets Every desktop panel becomes a sheet with a drag handle and its own snap height — Add nearly full, Light about two thirds, Pieces short. Tap the dimmed room or the handle to dismiss.
Gestures One finger orbits, pinch zooms, two fingers pan, drag on a selected piece moves it, long-press-and-drag lifts it. Stated once under the camera chip instead of hidden in a help modal.
